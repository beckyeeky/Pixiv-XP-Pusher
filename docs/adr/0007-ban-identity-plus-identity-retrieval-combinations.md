# Ban identity-plus-identity retrieval combinations

Retrieval may combine Feature tags with Feature, Character, or Copyright tags, and may also fall back to single Character-only or Copyright-only searches for their own motive lanes. It must not combine Character plus Copyright, Character plus Character, or Copyright plus Copyright in the same retrieval query, because identity tags naturally co-occur and stacking them would repeatedly drag ranking and slate composition back into the same narrow identity cluster.
